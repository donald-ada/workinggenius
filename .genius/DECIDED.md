# Decided

The index of this repo's settled decisions — one line per decision, each
pointing at the fight that settled it (`decision-record` skill). Overturning
one moves its line to the new fight; the records stay as written.

- **Concept-first prose — detailed constraints and gate checklists retired** —
  model capability keeps rising; constraints written for yesterday's models
  bind tomorrow's judgment. Each skill keeps its purpose, its failure mode, one
  threshold. [The fight](../evals/RESULTS.md#2026-08-06--user-ruling-concept-first-redesign--detailed-constraints-and-gate-checklists-retired)
- **No sizing tiers, no hands-off modes — checkpoints are live** — the staged
  flow's own forward pressure reproduced the measured momentum failure, and a
  model running on its own approval finds no problems. Invocation is the
  decision to run the flow whole. [The fight](../evals/RESULTS.md#2026-08-07--user-ruling-sizing-and-hands-off-modes-retired-stage-thresholds-moved-last-and-made-evidence-bearing)
- **Prose-only — no hooks, no runtime, no CI, no state a fork can't carry** —
  the mechanical layer was deleted green (history to `925accf` is the receipt);
  single-maintainer honesty is a standing constraint. [The fight](../evals/RESULTS.md#2026-08-06--user-ruling-concept-first-redesign--detailed-constraints-and-gate-checklists-retired)
- **No new stages — the pressure toward less lives at the door** — work too
  small for six stages stays out of the flow entirely, rather than running it
  shallow. [The fight](../ROADMAP.md#standing-constraints)
- **No number ships ahead of its RESULTS row** — anything that sounds like a
  measurement traces to `evals/RESULTS.md` or it's a bug in the README.
  [The fight](../ROADMAP.md#standing-constraints)
- **Native redesign over ported prose** — mechanisms borrowed from other
  suites are re-derived from this plugin's own principles (the stage frame,
  kill-reasons, evidence expiry) rather than translated; the ported versions
  of decision-record and diagnose were replaced the week they landed, and the
  redesigns graded 12/12 where a ported row had failed.
  [The fight](../evals/RESULTS.md#2026-08-14--trigger-sweep-after-the-native-redesign-1212-on-sonnet)
