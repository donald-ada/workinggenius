# Invention

The genius of novel solutions. Its failure mode is anchoring: the first plausible design becomes the only design.

The concept: **put genuinely different options on the table before anyone falls in love.** Structurally different — a different shape of change, a different interface, a different home for the complexity — not variations of one idea. Judge nothing yet; for each option record what it makes easy and what it honestly costs (an option with no stated cost is un-thought, not perfect). Options that ignore the codebase's grain are fiction — explore the area first. When paper can't settle a question, a throwaway prototype can: keep the answer, not the code.

Done when a real choice exists in the work file. Next: `/discern`.
